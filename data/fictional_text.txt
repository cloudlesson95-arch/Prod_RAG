The fictional town of Oakhaven is famous for its annual Pumpkin Festival. 
In 2025, the festival drew 14,250 visitors over three days in October. 
The mayor, Elena Rostova, announced a new city budget of $2.4 million for downtown improvements. 
However, local business owner Marcus Vance complained that parking remains the primary issue for shoppers. 
To fix this, the city council voted 5 to 2 on November 12 to build a new multi-level garage near Maple Street. 
Construction begins in spring 2027 and will cost $850,000.
